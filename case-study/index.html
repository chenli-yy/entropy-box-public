Nine compiled topics, in full

Aggregate counts are easy to publish and hard to trust. These nine topics are reproduced completely — every task chain and step, every capability with its full description, every implementing repository, the task-chain diagram, and a summary of the retrieval ledgers the record was built from. Together they are a fraction of a percent of the artifact, published so the structure can be inspected rather than taken on trust.

On language. Capability and asset names are bilingual (English + Chinese). Capability descriptions are in Chinese, which is the source language of the compiled corpus. We publish them as they are rather than machine-translating them, because the descriptions carry the engineering detail — inputs, outputs, constraints, performance envelopes — that a translation pass would blur.
